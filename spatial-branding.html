07 // SPRINT METHODOLOGY

Every great space begins with an idea.

A structured 6-phase engineering workflow that keeps your physical build on schedule and aligned with brand vision.

01 // PHASE WEEK 01

Understand

Deep intake on business model, target customer profile, site dimensions, foot-traffic analysis, and functional operational constraints.

02 // PHASE WEEK 02

Define

Synthesizing the core brand narrative into physical experience principles, spatial storytelling pillars, and visual moodboards.

03 // PHASE WEEKS 03-04

Conceptualise

Developing zoning layouts, customer journey choreography, sightlines, entry reveals, and initial 3D volumetric massing.

04 // PHASE WEEKS 05-06

Design

Refining identity graphics, custom signage typography, material palettes, finish schedules, and photorealistic 3D renders.

05 // PHASE WEEKS 07-08

Develop

Producing detailed graphic dielines, material specification binders, lighting temperature specs, and fabrication handoff packages.

06 // PHASE LAUNCH PHASE

Coordinate

Collaborating directly with your architects, interior designers, general contractors, and signmakers through final punch-list handover.
