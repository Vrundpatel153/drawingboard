Questions
founders ask

How are you different from a big agency?

You work directly with the senior team that designs your brand. No account managers, no hand-offs, and no more than two or three active clients per sprint.

How long does it take?

A Brand-to-Shelf sprint runs 4 to 6 weeks, from discovery to production-ready files. Brand-to-Market, with the website, runs 6 to 8.

What do we get at the end?

A logo system, colour and type tokens, a brand guidelines PDF, production specs for paper, foils and finishes, social templates and every source file. You own all of it.

What does it cost?

Fixed-scope sprints starting at ₹4,75,000. SaaS and platform work is quoted by scope. No hourly meter, no retainer creep.

Can you refresh a brand we already have?

Yes. We audit first, keep what’s earning its place and redesign what isn’t.

Do you build the website too?

Yes. Identity, packaging and web under one roof (React, Shopify or Framer), so the brand holds together from shelf to screen.
