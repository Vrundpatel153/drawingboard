03 / Good questions

Before we begin.

Is the starting price the final price?

It is the starting investment for the agreed base engagement. Additional deliverables, extra SKU adaptations, and technical complexity are quoted transparently before work begins.

Can I combine services?

Yes. Our most popular engagement is the Brand-to-Market system, combining strategic identity, master packaging, and custom web development under one unified proposal.

How many revisions are included?

Structured revision milestones are built into every sprint phase. Approval gates are clearly defined in your written proposal to ensure precision without endless cycles.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. We regularly partner with brands across the US, UK, UAE, Europe, and India. International engagements receive a tailored proposal quoted in USD or GBP.

When can we start?

We limit active engagements to 2–3 client sprints at any given time to preserve senior creative focus. Availability and kick-off dates are confirmed during our initial discovery call.
