03 / Good questions

Before we begin.

Is the starting price the final price?

It is the transparent starting investment for the agreed base engagement sprint. Additional deliverables, extra SKU adaptations, or complex features are clearly quoted before work begins.

Can I combine services into a connected sprint?

Yes. Our flagship Brand-to-Market Suite (₹4,75,000 / $4,960) unifies strategic brand identity, master packaging with SKU adaptations, and custom responsive web design & development into one cohesive engagement.

How many revisions and concepts are included?

Each sprint includes structured collaborative milestones. We explore distinct creative routes in Phase 1, followed by refined development rounds and final artwork polish. All review rounds are specified in your proposal.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. Over 40% of our clients operate across the US, UK, UAE, Europe, and Australia. We work smoothly across timezones with tailored proposals in USD, GBP, or EUR and clear digital review cadences.

When can we start?

To maintain senior creative leadership and rigorous quality, we limit intake to 2–3 active client sprints per cycle. We confirm availability and project timelines during our initial discovery call.
