Questions
founders ask

How are you different from a traditional agency?

You work directly with the senior design engineers who craft your brand. No account managers, no junior dilution, no bloated agency markups, and no more than two or three active clients per sprint.

How long does a brand sprint take?

Our core Brand-to-Shelf sprint runs 4 to 6 weeks from kickoff to production-ready files. Full Brand-to-Market engagements (with custom web engineering) span 6 to 8 weeks.

What deliverables do we get at handover?

Complete intellectual property ownership, mathematical master logos, color tokens, comprehensive guidelines book, print-ready dieline vector files with substrate/foil callouts, social templates, and every raw vector source file.

What is your pricing model?

Fixed-scope sprint pricing with 100% upfront transparency: Brand Identity starting at ₹3,50,000 / $3,650; Brand + Packaging + Web suite at ₹4,75,000 / $4,960. No hourly billing, no surprise change orders.

Can you refresh or elevate an existing brand?

Yes. We audit what you have, preserve what is earning its place in the market, and re-engineer what is holding you back from commanding your category.

Do you build the website and packaging under one roof?

Yes. Identity, structural packaging, and high-performance digital engineering are built together so the brand experience never fragments.
